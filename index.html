Artisan Heritage • Est. 1965

The Living Craft of Sanganeri Block Printing

The name "Chhipa" (छीपा) originates from the ancestral Rajasthani community of master stampers who have guarded the secret alchemy of wooden block carving, natural mordants, and indigo vats for over 500 years along the banks of Sanganer's waters.

01 The Carving

Teak Wood Block Carving

Seasoned teak and *Sheesham* wood are carved by hand using miniature steel chisels. Master carvers engrave the Rekh (fine outline block) and separate Datta (filler color blocks) with micro-pin alignment so multiple colors register seamlessly.

✓ Pin-point relief engraving • Hand-drilled air vents
02 The Alchemy

Natural & Rapid Dye Preparation

Raw fabrics undergo Harda (myrobalan) treatment to absorb dyes evenly. We brew fermented indigo vats, iron-rust Kashish for deep greys, and madder roots for vibrant Rajasthani terracotta reds. Dyes are blended with natural tamarind paste for crisp stamping.

✓ Fermented indigo vats • Non-toxic skin-safe mordants
03 The Rhythm

Hand Stamping & Sun Curing

Fabrics are stretched taut over 20-meter padded tables. Our artisans strike each block rhythmically using the heel of their hand. Once stamped, the cotton is washed in mineral-rich Sanganer water to bloom the colors, followed by open-air sun curing under the Rajasthan sun.

✓ 1,200+ hand stamps per 10 meters • River washed & sun dried
