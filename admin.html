Live Google Sheets Cloud Sync

Connect a published Google Sheet CSV link. When you update prices or inventory on your phone via Google Sheets, changes automatically sync to your customers' browsers!

💡
Crucial Developer Tip for Global Deployment:

Pasting the CSV link in this dashboard saves it in your browser's localStorage. To ensure every single new customer who visits your website worldwide automatically fetches from your Google Sheet on load without needing manual setup, paste your output=csv link directly into DEFAULT_GOOGLE_SHEET_CSV_URL at line 22 of assets/js/store-config.js before deploying to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.

How to publish a Google Sheet in 30 seconds:

1. Open your Google Sheet containing columns: id, title, price, tag, instock, moq, image

2. Click File → Share → Publish to web.

3. Select Entire Document (or Sheet 1) and change "Web page" to Comma-separated values (.csv).

4. Click Publish, copy the generated link, and paste it into the box above!
